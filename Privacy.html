Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to SevrisseOS. Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains how SevrisseOS accesses and handles information when you use the application.

2. Camera Permission and Air Gesture

Air Gesture processes camera data locally. Camera frames used for gesture recognition are processed on your Android device and are not transmitted to SevrisseOS servers.

SevrisseOS requests camera permission to enable its Air Gesture functionality. This allows the application to detect and recognize hand movements.

  • Camera access is used for gesture recognition.
  • Gesture processing takes place on the device.
  • Camera frames are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Camera permission can be revoked through Android settings.

3. Information Accessed by the Application

Depending on the features you use, SevrisseOS may access certain information required for its operation, including:

  • Installed application information for launcher functionality.
  • Camera input when Air Gesture is enabled.
  • Application preferences and configuration settings.

Information required for local launcher functionality is handled on your device.

4. Data Storage

Application preferences and configuration may be stored locally on your Android device. Air Gesture camera processing takes place locally.

5. Internet and Data Transmission

Air Gesture does not require camera frames to be transmitted over the internet. SevrisseOS does not upload Air Gesture camera frames to our servers.

This statement specifically concerns Air Gesture camera processing and does not imply that every optional or future feature is necessarily offline.

6. Third-Party Services

SevrisseOS does not authorize third parties to receive Air Gesture camera frames from the application. If third-party services are introduced in future versions, this policy will be updated to explain their relevant data practices.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings. You may revoke camera access whenever you choose, although doing so will disable Air Gesture functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

SevrisseOS is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children through the application.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as SevrisseOS develops new features or changes its data practices. Updates will be published on this page.
